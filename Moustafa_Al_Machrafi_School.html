About Our School

Building Excellence
Since Day One

Moustafa Al Machrafi Middle School stands as a beacon of academic excellence in Oujda, Morocco. Our institution combines rigorous academics with a nurturing environment, ensuring every student reaches their full potential.

We believe in holistic education — developing not just intellect, but character, creativity, and civic responsibility in every young person who walks through our doors.
